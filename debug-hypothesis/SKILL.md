## Format
`H1: cause`
`For: evidence`
`Against: evidence`
`Test: distinguishing experiment`
`Cost: low/medium/high`
`Status: unknown/supported/eliminated`

Keep roughly 2–4 high-value hypotheses. Remove contradicted hypotheses immediately. Never patch on plausibility alone.
